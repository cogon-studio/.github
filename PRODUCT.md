## Products

- **Lean Photos** — iOS Expo client. Media stays on the device. Clear commits through Apple into Recently Deleted and cannot be restored by the app.
- **Local Git** — iOS 26. Syncs an On My iPhone folder with a writable GitHub repository. No force-push or rebase.
- **Podspace** — Desktop. Websites become isolated windows, grouped in Spaces, separated by Profiles.
